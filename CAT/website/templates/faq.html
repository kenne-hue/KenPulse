Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report an issue?

To report an issue, click on the "Report Now" button in the navigation menu. You'll need to provide a title, description, location, and optionally attach a photo of the issue.

How can I track my reports?

You can track your reports by clicking on "Track Reports" in the navigation menu. This will show you all your submitted reports and their current status.

What types of issues can I report?

You can report various urban issues including potholes, broken streetlights, garbage accumulation, damaged infrastructure, and other public concerns.

How long does it take to resolve an issue?

Resolution time varies depending on the type and severity of the issue. You'll receive updates on your report's status through the platform.

Is my personal information secure?

Yes, we take your privacy seriously. Your personal information is protected according to our Privacy Policy, and we only share necessary details with relevant authorities.

Can I report issues anonymously?

No, you need to create an account to report issues. This helps us maintain accountability and allows us to keep you updated on the status of your reports.
